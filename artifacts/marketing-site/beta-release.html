Ball Boi Beta Footage Release

Version 2 · November 2026

You're helping test Ball Boi before it launches. This explains what happens to the matches you record.

Who can join. You must be 18 or older.

What's filmed. When you record with Ball Boi, your phone films the whole court with its back camera. That includes you, your opponent and anyone else in view. The microphone listens for the sound of the ball being hit.

What we do with it.

  • Analysis: we turn your match into stats and a simulated replay.
  • Testing: we may hand-label your match (shot type, in or out, how each point ended) and use it to measure how accurate Ball Boi is.
  • Training: we use the video to train our shot tracking only if every player in the match left "Help Ball Boi get smarter" on. Opponents who aren't on Ball Boi are never used for training.

What we never do. Video is never shown in the app, and it never leaves Ball Boi's own systems. Companies that store or process data for us, like our cloud storage provider, handle it only on our behalf. We never sell your footage or personal information. Audio is used only to detect shots and is never played back. During the beta, we keep it for up to 30 days while we choose matches for testing. If we pick yours, we keep its audio until Ball Boi launches to re-check our accuracy, then delete it. Otherwise it's deleted after 30 days.

Filming an opponent who isn't in the beta. Before you record, you confirm they agreed to be filmed. Please ask them first.

How long we keep it. Beta footage is kept until Ball Boi launches. After that, raw video is deleted after analysis, and a training copy is kept only while every player leaves training on.

Withdrawing. You can leave anytime. Email support@ballboi.tennis or delete your account in Settings. Within 30 days we delete all of your video, including any copies used for testing or training. Shot labels we made by hand stay, with your name removed. Models we've already trained aren't rebuilt.

Changes. If we change this release, the app asks you to agree again before your next recording.
