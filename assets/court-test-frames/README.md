# Court test frames (John, Nov 8)

Real footage from John's first court test of the voice coach. The app couldn't find the court and the coach's advice didn't make sense.

- `beacon-pickleball-video-01..10.jpg`: one frame per second from a 10-second iPhone Camera clip, turned upright (the file was tagged portrait with the court sideways), 960 x 540.
- `beacon-pickleball-still-1..2.jpg`: two screenshots John took of the same clip, turned upright.

The view: green hard court, white tennis lines with yellow pickleball lines painted over them, filmed at standing height from just behind the near baseline. The far baseline is hidden behind the net from this height. People are on court in most frames.

Use these as test cases for `lib/court/detect.ts` and `lib/court/aim.ts`. Don't copy them into the app bundle. Only scaled-down copies belong in test fixtures.
