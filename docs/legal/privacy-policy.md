# Privacy Policy

_Draft for legal review. Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE]._

Ball Boi helps adults find tennis players nearby, run leagues, track stats, and analyze their matches. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

## Who can use Ball Boi

Ball Boi is for people 18 and older. We check age when you sign up and keep only a yes or no, never your birth date. If you're under 18, we don't create an account.

## What we collect

- **Account details:** your name, email address, and how you sign in (Apple, Google, or email). Sign-in is handled by our authentication provider, Clerk.
- **Player profile:** photo, Ball Boi Rating, dominant hand, bio, and phone number if you add one. If you link a rating account such as UTR, we receive your rating and player ID from that service.
- **Home area:** a zip code or your approximate location, used to show players, leagues, and courts near you. We ask for location only while you use the app, and you can use a zip code instead.
- **League and match activity:** leagues you join or run, availability, match requests, schedules, scores, standings, and stats.
- **Messages:** messages you send to other players and in league chats.
- **Match video and audio:** when you record a match with Ball Boi, we upload the video to analyze it. Audio is used only to detect when the ball is hit. Video may include other players on court.
- **Purchases:** whether you subscribe to Ball Boi Pro. Payment is handled by Apple; we never see your card.
- **Device data:** push notification tokens, so we can send the alerts you've turned on.

We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## How we use it

- To run your account, leagues, schedules, standings, and matchups.
- To analyze your match video, detect shots, and build your replay and stats.
- To improve shot tracking (see "Training on match video" below).
- To send reminders and messages you've turned on.
- To keep the community safe, including reviewing reports of abuse.

## Match video, audio, and retention

- **Replays are simulated.** Ball Boi shows a court animation built from shot data. Match video is never played back in the app.
- **Raw video is deleted after analysis,** unless the match is kept for training.
- **Audio is never played back** and is deleted after the match is processed.

## Training on match video

We use match video to train our shot tracking. This is on by default and you can turn it off anytime in Settings. A match is used for training only if both players left it on. Matches against someone who doesn't have a Ball Boi account are never used for training unless that person joins and leaves it on. Video used for training stays within Ball Boi's systems. Your personal information is never shared or sold.

## Who can see what

- **Other players** see your name, photo, rating, and player card highlights. Your error patterns and heatmaps stay private.
- **League members** see results and standings in leagues you join.
- **Your replays** are visible to you and the other player in that match.
- **Service providers** process data for us under contract and can't use it for their own purposes: Clerk (sign-in), Cloudflare (hosting and video storage), Stream (messaging), Anthropic (helps review reported messages), RevenueCat (subscription status), and our database host.
- **Rating partners:** if you link UTR or a similar service, we share only what's needed to link your account. You can unlink it from Profile.
- **The law:** we may disclose data if the law requires it.

## Safety and moderation

When someone reports a message or player, a person on our team reviews it, with help from an AI review tool if we haven't acted within 6 hours. Reviews look at the reported content and account history. We act on every report within 24 hours.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account anytime from **Profile > Delete account**. That permanently deletes your profile, photo, contact details, messages, ratings, and every match you recorded, including the video, and removes your matches from future training. Scores from league matches you played stay in other players' history, listed as "Deleted player," so their records stay accurate.
